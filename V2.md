# Offering v2 — geography and the people in the room

V2 is an opt-in experience at `/v2` (the password-protected wall) and `/v2/join` (the public participant view). The original `/` and `/join` experience remains available. Use matching wall and participant URLs: the two versions have separate rooms.

## Experience

Participants give a name or stay anonymous, choose a local place on the map or through search, review what will be shared, and set their screen aside. The wall's side rail first shows that they joined, then that their place is held, and finally when its connection is appearing. These are contribution states, not live location or online-presence tracking.

The local view begins at Whitman. One continuous pullback reveals the geographic connections. Begin, hold and continue keep the same control position. Dragging the progress rail moves backward or forward and leaves the shared timeline held. Selecting a person pauses that timeline, frames their place and its relationship to the room, and reveals its source and precision. Return to the room restores the shared view.

The presenter opens the hometown invitation separately. Phones switch when visible, without another login. A member's hometown and local place share one public member ID. At world scale, actual coastlines and great-circle paths form the globe. The globe turns toward distant arrivals and rotates slowly after the reveal; hold globe stops that ambient motion. Reduced-motion preferences stop orbital motion and use camera steps.

The More menu includes assisted entry, replay, hiding controls, full screen, the participant link, and clearing this v2 room. In an empty room it also offers **rehearse with example places**. Rehearsal is explicit and stays labeled **example room**; it never replaces participants who already joined.

## What the map displays

| View | Data and rendering | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| quiet | Restrained OpenFreeMap/OpenStreetMap vector geography | Simplified cartography, not photographic terrain |
| detail | More geographic labels and detail; MapLibre building extrusions on WebGL2-capable browsers | Building shapes come from the map data; they are not photographed facades or a surveyed 3D campus model |
| aerial | NYS natural-color orthoimagery, correctly aligned to the local geographic view | Local Syracuse-area view only; capture dates vary by county |
| globe | Natural Earth 1:110m land geometry and great-circle connections to submitted hometowns | Geographic overview, not satellite imagery |

The renderer uses the already bundled OpenLayers Canvas implementation when WebGL2 is unavailable. The globe also uses Canvas. This preserves a working map on browsers that failed to open the original WebGL-only implementation.

### Aerial source, checked 6 November 2026

The [NYS program](https://gis.ny.gov/orthoimagery) makes its imagery available through public web services. Its [FAQ](https://gis.ny.gov/orthoimagery-faqs) states that these are available at no cost. V2 uses the **Latest** mosaic, credited to **NYS ITS Geospatial Services**. The [published service metadata](https://orthos.its.ny.gov/arcgis/rest/services/wms/Latest/MapServer/info/iteminfo?f=pjson) describes a 2022–2025 mosaic at approximately 12-inch service resolution. Published Syracuse/Onondaga imagery is spring 2022; a newer flight listing does not mean that imagery is already available in the service.

The configured 40-mile rectangle is a display bound. The server separately checks the actual distance from Whitman. Requests use ordinary browser HTTP caching. The app keeps only a bounded in-memory image cache, does not bulk-download imagery, and falls back to vector geography when imagery fails.

Fully photorealistic 3D is a separate future integration. This release does not use paid imagery keys, invent photographic detail, or add a new billing account.

## Data quality

- Search prioritizes the place name, then distance; local results must be within 40 miles. Streets are excluded from ordinary place-name queries. Hometown search keeps actual settlements and respects state/country qualifiers.
- Provider IDs and nearby repeated records are deduplicated. Locality and street context help distinguish branches.
- A search result carries a short-lived signed selection token. The server retains provider metadata only from a valid selection, bound to its point, label, round and origin. Client-supplied provider claims do not establish provenance.
- Search outages are reported as unavailable, with retry/map alternatives. Example places are never substituted for live search results.
- A home/address is reduced to a roughly 500 m neighborhood grid cell before durable storage. Its original address, precise point, provider ID and selection token are discarded. Both the review screen and the wall describe it as approximate.
- One browser can correct or remove its own place while the invitation is open. A public member ID connects its two rounds; its private participant cookie is not sent to other participants or to the presenter.
- Participant state contains only that browser's member and contributions plus room-level counts and phase. The complete list and controls require the existing wall session.

## Reversibility and deployment

The canonical app still lives in `contextation-offering/contextation-offering/`. The existing three Wrangler roots still resolve to that app. Worker name, `ROOM` binding, migration tag, encrypted `SITE_PASSWORD`, 9-character password minimum and asset gate are preserved.

New requests use `/api/v2/*` and the existing `Room` class in a **separate Durable Object instance**, `class-relational-v2`, with its own `room:relational:v1` snapshot. The original `class` instance and legacy storage keys are not migrated, cleared or rewritten.

The fastest fallback is simply to use `/` and `/join` again. To remove the experiment from a deployment, revert the v2 commit through Git; no data migration reversal is needed. A pre-v2 backup branch points to baseline commit `0446ba8c0082d12a3c8bb3d0d2157794dab52005`.

The only extra browser origin allowed for v2 map documents is `https://orthos.its.ny.gov`. The original map pages retain their original CSP. The participant page and its required modules are explicitly public; the wall and presenter scripts stay behind the password gate.

## Verification

Run `npm run check` from the repository root or canonical app. The suite includes the original authentication, room and deployment checks plus isolated v2 tests for member privacy, stable identity, old-room isolation, signed provenance, home approximation, search behavior, shared-clock seeking, concurrent joins, durable-write rollback and protected controls.

Wrangler dry-run bundles the actual Worker and static assets without deploying. Browser review should cover local and hometown submission, participant following, held progress seeking, aerial/vector rendering, globe rotation and narrow-screen layout. The local review harness uses synthetic names and its own disposable storage; it is not part of the shipped app.

For the classroom dry run, open `/v2` on the projector and `/v2/join` on a second phone. Test quiet/detail contrast on that projector, then clear the rehearsal room before inviting classmates. Clear the room again after the offering.

## Geographic dependencies

MapLibre 6.16.0 and the existing OpenLayers bundle are reused. `dist/v2/map-geo.js` bundles d3-geo 3.1.1, d3-array 3.2.5 and internmap 2.0.3; their ISC notices are in `map-geo.LICENSE`. `map-land.json` is public-domain Natural Earth land data via world-atlas 2.0.2. `map-geo-source.js` records the projection exports for rebuilding that bundle.
